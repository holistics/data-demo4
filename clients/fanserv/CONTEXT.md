# FanServ Season Board

This context describes live-sports events as media-planning candidates. It separates the sporting event, the team perspective used to evaluate it, and FanServ's relationship to the associated supply.

## Language

**Season Board**:
The media planner's continuous view of past-game evidence and future-game opportunity across the sports calendar.
_Avoid_: Stadium dashboard, trading desk

**Game**:
A scheduled sporting event between two teams. A game remains the same event when its schedule, score, broadcast, or planning signals change.
_Avoid_: Match, fixture

**Focal team**:
The team whose perspective is used to calculate a GamePulse evaluation. The same game can have one evaluation for each participating team.
_Avoid_: Selected team, primary team

**GamePulse evaluation**:
A modeled assessment of one game from one focal team's perspective at a point in time.
_Avoid_: Game score, forecast

**GamePulse score**:
The 1–100 result of a GamePulse evaluation. It directionally indicates expected attention and potential impression impact; it is not a forecast impression count.
_Avoid_: Impression forecast, popularity

**GamePulse component**:
A named contribution to a GamePulse evaluation, such as season type, time bucket, network exposure, rivalry, team quality, or season progress.
_Avoid_: Metric, factor

**Supply team**:
A team for which FanServ is authorized to monetize media inventory.
_Avoid_: Featured team, partner team

**Supply affiliation**:
The relationship between a game and a participating supply team. It indicates a potential FanServ supply path, not confirmed inventory availability.
_Avoid_: Inventory, booking

**Opportunity candidate**:
A future game with a supply affiliation and a GamePulse evaluation. It is not necessarily available or affordable until inventory and pricing data confirm that.
_Avoid_: Available game, buyable game

**Evidence game**:
A completed game whose observed performance can inform future planning decisions.
_Avoid_: Historical inventory

**Broadcast placement**:
One network, channel, medium, and locale combination carrying a game. A game can have multiple broadcast placements.
_Avoid_: Broadcast, network
